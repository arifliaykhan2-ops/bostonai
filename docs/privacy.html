Boson AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Boson AI (“we”, “us”) is an iOS app that turns your photos and text into AI-generated images and videos. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. Contact: omerbabaverdiyev@gmail.com.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data. If you sign in with Apple, we receive an Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email (which may be an Apple private relay address). If you sign in with email, we store your email address. Before you sign in, the app creates an anonymous guest account identified only by a random ID.
  • Content you provide. Photos, videos, text prompts and scripts you submit for generation.
  • Generated content. The images and videos the AI models create for you, and a history of your generations (prompt, chosen options, model, cost, time).
  • Purchases. Which credit packs or subscriptions you bought and your credit balance. Payments are processed by Apple; we never see your card details.
  • Push notification token. If you allow notifications, a device token so we can tell you when a video is ready.
  • Reports. If you report content, the item and the reason you chose.
  • Technical data. Basic logs our hosting providers keep to run and secure the service (for example IP address and request times).

We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

2. Why we use it

  • To generate the images and videos you ask for and show them in your gallery.
  • To run your account, credits and purchases, and restore purchases.
  • To send notifications you allowed (for example “your video is ready”).
  • To keep the service safe: rate limits, automated checks of prompts against our content rules, and reviewing reports.

Where EU/UK law applies, our legal bases are performing our contract with you (generation, account, purchases), your consent (sending your photos and prompts to AI providers, which you agree to in the app before your first generation, and notifications), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

3. Who receives your data

We share data only with the service providers we need to run the app:

  • Supabase — database, file storage, sign-in and server functions.
  • fal.ai — runs the AI models. Your photos, videos, prompts and scripts are sent to fal.ai and to the company that makes the model or template you pick, which can include Black Forest Labs (Flux), Google (Nano Banana, Gemini), OpenAI (GPT Image), ByteDance (Seedance, Seedream), Kuaishou (Kling), MiniMax, Alibaba (Wan), xAI (Grok), ShengShu (Vidu), PixVerse, Ideogram, Recraft, Lightricks (LTX) and ElevenLabs (voices for Talking Photo).
  • OpenRouter / Anthropic (through fal.ai) — automated check of your text prompts against our content rules.
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, App Store payments and push notifications; Expo — delivers push notifications.

These providers process data on our behalf and only to provide their service. Some of them are located outside your country, including in the United States; where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

4. Community content

The Explore feed shows public posts from Civitai, with a link to each original post and its author. When you open one, your device loads it from Civitai’s servers.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your account, uploads, generations and history until you delete them or delete your account. When you delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account), we delete your account, your uploaded and generated files and your history, and, if you used Sign in with Apple, we revoke the app’s access to your Apple ID. Purchase records may be kept where tax or accounting law requires. AI providers keep the data they receive only as long as their own policies allow for running the request.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Withdraw consent to AI processing at any time in Profile; generation is then turned off.
  • Delete individual generations, or your whole account, in the app.
  • Turn notifications off in iOS Settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to some processing. Email us and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

Boson AI is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Your uploads and results are stored privately and are only reachable through short-lived signed links.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.

10. Contact
